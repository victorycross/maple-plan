# Last Will and Testament — Sample Template (Canada)

> ⚠️ **Educational template only.** A will is a legally binding document. Have it drafted or reviewed by a Canadian lawyer or notary (in Quebec, a notary), and signed in accordance with the witness rules of your province. This template does NOT replace legal advice.

---

## I, [FULL LEGAL NAME],
of [ADDRESS, CITY, PROVINCE, POSTAL CODE], being of sound mind, declare this to be my Last Will and Testament, revoking all prior wills and codicils.

## 1. Executor (Estate Trustee in Ontario / Liquidator in Quebec)

I appoint **[PRIMARY EXECUTOR NAME]**, of [ADDRESS], as my Executor. If they are unable or unwilling to act, I appoint **[ALTERNATE EXECUTOR NAME]**, of [ADDRESS], in their place.

I authorize my Executor to retain professionals (lawyers, accountants, financial advisors) at the expense of my estate.

## 2. Guardianship of Minor Children (if applicable)

If at my death any of my children are under the age of majority in my province, I appoint **[GUARDIAN NAME]**, of [ADDRESS], as their guardian, and **[ALTERNATE GUARDIAN]** as alternate.

## 3. Specific Bequests

I give the following specific gifts:

- To **[NAME]**: [DESCRIPTION OF ITEM OR AMOUNT]
- To **[CHARITY]**: $[AMOUNT] or [DESCRIPTION]
- [add as needed]

## 4. Residue of Estate

I give the residue of my estate (everything not specifically gifted above) to **[SPOUSE / BENEFICIARY NAME]** if they survive me by 30 days, otherwise equally among my children **[NAMES]** per stirpes.

## 5. Powers of Executor

I authorize my Executor to:

- Pay all just debts, funeral expenses, and taxes from my estate
- Sell, lease, or transfer any of my property
- Make distributions in cash or in kind, at fair market value
- Make tax elections (e.g., capital-gains spousal rollover, RRSP/RRIF designations)
- Carry on or wind up any business interests
- Engage professional advisors

## 6. Funeral and Burial Wishes

I express the following non-binding wishes regarding my funeral, burial, or cremation: [DETAILS, OR REFER TO SEPARATE LETTER OF WISHES]

## 7. Digital Assets

I direct my Executor to access and manage my digital assets (email accounts, cloud storage, social media, cryptocurrency, etc.) as outlined in my separate Digital Asset Inventory.

---

**Signed** this _____ day of __________, 20___, in [CITY, PROVINCE].

Signature of Testator: _____________________________

**Witnesses** (two adults, not beneficiaries or spouses of beneficiaries):

Witness 1: ________________________ Address: ________________________

Witness 2: ________________________ Address: ________________________

---

### Provincial signing rules (verify yours):

- **Ontario, BC, Alberta, etc.**: 2 adult witnesses present at the same time. Beneficiaries cannot be witnesses.
- **Quebec**: Notarial will (preferred — exempt from probate) requires a notary and 1 witness. Holograph will (handwritten, signed) needs no witnesses but is provable in court.
- **Holograph wills** (entirely in your handwriting, signed): valid in most provinces except BC.
- **Update after**: marriage, divorce, birth of children, major asset changes, moves to another province.

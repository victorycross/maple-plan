# Power of Attorney for Property — Sample Template (Canada)

> ⚠️ **Educational template only.** A Power of Attorney is a legally binding document. Names, witnessing rules, and capacity tests vary significantly by province. Have a Canadian lawyer or notary draft or review it before signing.

## Provincial names

| Jurisdiction | Document name |
|---|---|
| Ontario | Continuing Power of Attorney for Property |
| BC | Enduring Power of Attorney |
| Alberta | Enduring Power of Attorney |
| Quebec | Mandate in Anticipation of Incapacity (Mandat de protection) — must be notarized |
| Nova Scotia, NB, PEI, NL | Enduring Power of Attorney |
| Manitoba, Saskatchewan | Enduring Power of Attorney |

---

## I, [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]
of [ADDRESS, CITY, PROVINCE, POSTAL CODE], being of sound mind, appoint:

**Primary Attorney**: [NAME], of [ADDRESS], [RELATIONSHIP]

**Alternate Attorney** (if primary cannot act): [NAME], of [ADDRESS], [RELATIONSHIP]

## 1. Powers Granted

I authorize my Attorney to do anything that I could lawfully do by attorney, including:

- Operate, open, and close bank accounts
- Buy, sell, or mortgage real estate (other than my principal residence without my written consent)
- Manage investments, including buying and selling securities
- File and pay taxes (including signing my T1)
- Apply for government benefits on my behalf (CPP, OAS, GIS, provincial disability supports)
- Pay debts, including mortgages, taxes, and household bills
- Make gifts and charitable donations consistent with my prior pattern of giving

## 2. When This Power Takes Effect

☐ **Immediately** (continues if I lose capacity)

☐ **Only when I become incapable** of managing property, as confirmed by [TWO PHYSICIANS / A CAPACITY ASSESSOR]

## 3. Restrictions

[OPTIONAL — e.g., "My Attorney shall not sell my home at [ADDRESS] without the written agreement of my children."]

## 4. Compensation

☐ My Attorney shall serve without compensation other than reasonable out-of-pocket expenses.

☐ My Attorney may take the compensation permitted by [PROVINCIAL STATUTE].

## 5. Revocation

This Power of Attorney revokes all prior Powers of Attorney for Property.

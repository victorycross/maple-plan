# Power of Attorney for Personal Care — Sample Template (Canada)

> ⚠️ **Educational template only.** This document gives someone authority to make healthcare and personal-care decisions when you cannot. Provincial laws vary widely — have a Canadian lawyer review.

## Provincial names

| Jurisdiction | Document name |
|---|---|
| Ontario | Power of Attorney for Personal Care |
| BC | Representation Agreement (Section 7 or Section 9) + Advance Directive |
| Alberta | Personal Directive |
| Quebec | Mandate in Anticipation of Incapacity (also covers person) |
| Atlantic Canada | Personal Directive / Health Care Directive |
| Prairie provinces | Personal Directive / Health Care Directive |

---

## I, [YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]
of [ADDRESS, CITY, PROVINCE, POSTAL CODE], being of sound mind and at least the age of majority in my province, appoint:

**Primary Substitute Decision-Maker**: [NAME], of [ADDRESS], [RELATIONSHIP]

**Alternate**: [NAME], of [ADDRESS], [RELATIONSHIP]

## 1. Powers Granted

I authorize my SDM to make decisions about my:

- Health care, medical treatment, and dental care
- Admission to long-term care, hospital, or hospice
- Personal-care needs: nutrition, shelter, clothing, hygiene, safety
- Access to my medical records (PHIPA / PIPEDA / equivalent)

## 2. My Care Wishes (Advance Directive)

I express the following preferences. They are intended to guide — not bind — my SDM.

### Life-prolonging treatment

☐ I want all reasonable life-prolonging treatment.

☐ I want comfort-only care if I am terminally ill, in a persistent vegetative state, or have severe and irreversible dementia.

☐ I want CPR / mechanical ventilation withheld in the following circumstances: ___

### Pain management

I want pain managed aggressively even if doing so may shorten my life.

### Organ and tissue donation

☐ I wish to donate organs and tissues for transplantation.
☐ I wish to donate my body to medical science (specify institution: ___).
☐ I do not wish to donate.

### Religious / cultural considerations

[OPTIONAL]

### Place of death

I prefer to die at: ☐ Home  ☐ Hospital  ☐ Hospice  ☐ No preference

### Medical Assistance in Dying (MAID)

[OPTIONAL — Express your wishes; consult current federal MAID law]

## 3. When This Power Takes Effect

This authority is exercisable only when I am mentally incapable of making the relevant personal-care decision, as determined under applicable provincial law.
